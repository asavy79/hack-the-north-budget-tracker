
# 💰 Savvy Saver

**Savvy Saver** is a gamified personal finance app built during a hackathon to help students and young adults tackle bad spending habits and develop better saving behavior.

---

## 🎯 Inspiration

As students, we noticed a lack of accessible, high-quality investing and saving tools for people with limited financial resources. Managing money can be stressful and discouraging—Savvy Saver aims to make the experience fun, motivating, and beginner-friendly.

---

## 🚀 What It Does

- 📝 **Personalized Questionnaire:**  
  New users answer a brief set of questions to help the app understand their financial habits.

- 👹 **Identify Your Financial "Demons":**  
  Based on the user's banking history, the app generates three unique "demons"—representing their bad spending habits.

- ✅ **Goal Selection & Weekly Missions:**  
  Users pick one habit to tackle. The app sets a personalized, AI-generated weekly mission to overcome that habit.

- 📊 **Progress Dashboard:**  
  Users track their progress over the week with an interactive dashboard.

- 🏆 **Gamified Rewards:**  
  Completing a mission earns points that users can redeem for real RBC Loyalty points.

---

## 🛠️ How We Built It

- **Frontend:** React + Tailwind CSS for a responsive and user-friendly interface.
- **Backend & AI Services:**  
  Used **Cohere AI** for generating personalized goals and financial reports.
- **Authentication & Database:**  
  Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for managing users and storing data.
- **Banking Data & Rewards:**  
  Integrated with the **RBC API** to fetch user transactions and manage loyalty point redemptions.

---
